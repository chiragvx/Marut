Fly the Tejas over India. In your browser.

A free combat flight simulator. Take off from Bathinda in Punjab or INS Hansa in Goa, fly where you like, or take on another fighter. No download, no sign-up.

Desktop · keyboard · Chrome, Edge or Firefox

Two ways to fly

Mode

Free Flight

Start in a hardened shelter, on the runway or in the air. Pick the time of day, the weather and what hangs under the wings, then fly for as long as you like.

 Mode

Missions

Air-to-air: find the enemy on radar, lock on and shoot it down. Three missions: a one-on-one over the Punjab border, a scramble from the shelters against two bandits, and a duel off the Goa coast.
The aircraft

HAL Tejas Mk1A

India's single-engine delta-wing fighter, flown with a physics flight model and fly-by-wire limits.

Views
Cockpit, chase with free look, orbit and fly-by

Dawn to dusk, and after dark

Set the clock to any hour. Runways, taxiways and towns light up after dark, and the Tejas carries working lights: press L to step through them.

Where you fly

Bhisiana AFS, Bathinda

The main base: the Punjab plains near the border, with hardened aircraft shelters, a lit runway and taxi guidance to it.

INS Hansa, Goa Not fully tested

Coast, estuaries and headlands, with the Western Ghats inland. Still being tested: you may run into bugs here.

Runs on

  • A desktop or laptop browser with WebGL 2 (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
  • A keyboard; a mouse or gamepad is optional
  • Works offline once installed
  • Settings and progress stay on your device. No account.
